Clipador - Terms of Service

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clipador ("the app") is a personal desktop tool, run on its operator's own computer, that turns live streams and VODs of streamers who authorize clipping into short vertical videos, and posts the clips the operator approves to the operator's own social media accounts (YouTube and TikTok). By connecting an account to Clipador you agree to these terms.

1. Who can use it

Clipador is used by its operator to manage the operator's own accounts. It is not a public service: nobody else signs up, and no account other than the ones the operator connects is ever accessed.

2. Content and permissions

  • Only content from creators who authorized clipping is processed. The app records that authorization and gives visible credit to the original creator in every post.
  • Nothing is posted without the operator approving the clip first. Automatic scheduling only applies to clips the operator approved.
  • The operator is responsible for the content posted and for following TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Terms, and YouTube's Terms of Service.

3. Use of TikTok

Clipador uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API only to: identify the connected account, upload and publish the approved video with its caption and privacy setting, and read the basic statistics (views, likes, comments) of the videos it published. The operator can disconnect the account at any time in the app, and can also revoke access in TikTok's settings.

4. No warranty

The app is provided "as is", without warranties. The operator uses it at their own risk; uploads can fail or be delayed by the platforms.
