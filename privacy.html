Clipador - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clipador is a desktop tool that runs entirely on its operator's own computer. It has no servers, no user database in the cloud, and does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone.

1. Data we access from TikTok

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic): the account's open ID and display name, to show which account is connected.
  • Publishing (video.publish): to upload the clips the operator approved, with their caption and privacy setting, and to check the upload status.
  • Video list (video.list): view, like and comment counts of the videos Clipador published, so the operator can see which clips performed well.

2. Where the data is stored

The OAuth tokens and the statistics are stored only in a local database on the operator's computer. They are never sent to any third party. Videos are sent only to the platform the operator chose (TikTok or YouTube), through their official APIs.

3. Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in Clipador deletes its tokens immediately. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings (Security > Apps and services). Deleting the app's data folder removes everything.

4. Children

Clipador is not directed to children and is used only by its adult operator.

Clipador - Política de Privacidade

Atualizado em 18 de novembro de 2026

O Clipador roda inteiramente no computador do operador: não tem servidores, não guarda dados na nuvem e não vende nem compartilha dados com ninguém.

  • Do TikTok, acessa apenas: ID e nome da conta conectada; a publicação dos clipes aprovados; e as views, curtidas e comentários dos vídeos que ele mesmo publicou.
  • Tokens e estatísticas ficam só no banco de dados local do computador do operador.
  • Desconectar a conta no Clipador apaga os tokens na hora; o acesso também pode ser revogado nas configurações do TikTok.
  • Contato: caiorissa@gmail.com.
